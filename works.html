02 : Research

Computer Architecture • GPU Architecture • High-Performance Computing • Performance Analysis

Current

I'm a first-year PhD student at the University of Michigan, working with Prof. Reetuparna Das. I'm currently looking at how GPUs could support FP32-heavy HPC workloads by emulating FP32 in hardware with lower-precision (e.g., BF16) multipliers, and how that maps onto systolic arrays or CUDA-core-style vector units.
